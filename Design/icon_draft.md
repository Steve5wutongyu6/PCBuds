# Form

- Single centred earbud: round node + slightly angled stem, paper white on ink.
- Noise motif: three concentric arcs in body ink riding the outboard side of the stem.
- Body: graphite vertical gradient (0.263,0.271,0.298 -> 0.137,0.145,0.165), faint inner rim at 8.5% white.
- No text, no letters, no glow, no blur.

# Anti-goals

- Do not draw an OPPO/realme/OnePlus logo or any wordmark.
- Do not decorate with drop shadows heavier than the single 30% contact shadow.
